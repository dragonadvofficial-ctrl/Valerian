# Valerian — Care that goes further

An independent, responsive animal health website for Valerian, presented as a US animal health company in the supplied brief. The visual identity uses muted olive, sage and warm ivory, with a calm, clinical editorial style inspired by the Aeline reference.

## Preview
Serve the `dist` folder with any static HTTP server. The site has no runtime dependencies, build process or external CDN. Fonts, product images and photography are hosted locally.

## Features
- English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout and a locally remembered language preference.
- Five supplied products: NITROZAN, PhosphoVal 20%, OxyVal L.A, DIPHENEX and EroxVal 10%.
- Product filters, accessible detail dialogs, original package images and product-specific enquiry preparation.
- Separate information for livestock, horses and companion animals. Product use and eligible species are subject to each official label and veterinary guidance.
- Responsive mobile navigation, reduced-motion support, skip link, labelled forms and focus styling.
- EPGT appears only in the footer as the sole and exclusive official Middle East partner, as requested.

## Content and contact
Company copy follows the user-provided brief. Product summaries describe only the supplied package labels; they do not provide dosage or prescribing advice. Full composition, approved species, availability and use should be confirmed from official product documentation and a veterinarian.

No verified contact email or form destination was provided. The enquiry composer lets visitors copy or download an enquiry and does not claim to send it.

## Files
- `dist/index.html`: page structure.
- `dist/styles.css`: design system and responsive styling.
- `dist/app.js`: translations, product details and browser interactions.
- `dist/assets`: supplied product packaging, optimized photography, logo files and local fonts.
- `.openai/hosting.json`: identity for this independent Valerian Site, separate from Rigel.
- `vercel.json`: optional static deployment configuration.
